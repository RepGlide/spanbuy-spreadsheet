Parcel Weight Context Guide

Distinguish the terms before you read any weight. This page provides context, not quotes.

Weight is a context, not one number

When a parcel is being prepared, several different measures are in play. Reading them as one number is the fastest way to misread the situation. This page does not provide shipping quotes or a calculator.

Actual weight

The measured mass of the parcel. It is confirmed after items are packed, not read from a Sheet row.

Volumetric weight

A space-based estimate derived from how the items pack together. A light but bulky parcel can carry a high volumetric figure.

Packaging

The materials and grouping. Packaging changes both the actual weight and the volume, so it is confirmed together with them.

Route limits

What a chosen shipping route will and will not carry — size, material, or value limits. Confirmed against the route, not assumed from the Sheet.

A worked reading

  1. Actual weight is measured after the items are boxed.
  2. Volumetric weight is read from the packed dimensions.
  3. Packaging is noted because it changes both of the above.
  4. Route limits are checked against the chosen route.
